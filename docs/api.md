# API Reference (Endpoint Groups)

Base URL (local): `http://localhost:8081`

> For complete request/response schemas, use Swagger UI at `/swagger-ui/index.html`.

## Authentication & User Access

- `/api/v1/auth` - Authentication and account flows
- `/api/v1/users` - User management
- `/api/v1/role` - Role management
- `/api/v1/permission` - Permission management
- `/api/v1/permission-group` - Permission group management

## Catalog & Inventory

- `/api/v1/category` - Category management
- `/api/v1/brand` - Brand management
- `/api/v1/model` - Model management
- `/api/v1/variant-types` - Variant type management
- `/api/v1/variant-values` - Variant value management
- `/api/v1/product` - Product management
- `/api/v1/product-serial` - Product serial tracking
- `/api/v1/stock` - Stock operations
- `/api/v1/stores` - Store management

## Purchase & Suppliers

- `/api/v1/purchases` - Purchase operations
- `/api/v1/suppliers` - Supplier management
- `/api/v1/expenses` - Expense management
- `/api/v1/expense-types` - Expense type management

## Sales & Customers

- `/api/v1/customer` - Customer management
- `/api/v1/sales` - Sales operations
- `/api/v1/payments` - Payment management
- `/api/v1/quote` - Quote management

## Finance

- `/api/v1/currency` - Currency management
- `/api/v1/bank` - Bank management

## Reporting & Dashboard

- `/api/v1/dashboard` - Dashboard summaries
- `/api/v1/reports` - Sales/purchase/profit-loss reports
- `/api/v1/reports/expenses` - Expense reports
- `/api/v1/reports/product-serials` - Product serial reports

## File Management

- `/api/v1/files` - Upload, preview, and download files
